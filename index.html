About

I am a predoctoral researcher at the Becker Friedman Institute at the University of Chicago, working with Manasi Deshpande. Prior to this, I was an economist at the Reserve Bank of Australia (Australia's equivalent of the Federal Reserve). You can find my CV here.

My primary research interests are in labour and public economics, and I am interested in issues in household finance.
